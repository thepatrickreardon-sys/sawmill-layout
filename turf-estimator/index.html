Import from Moasure

In the Moasure app: File, Export, CSV. Load that file here. Units come from the file, and the biggest closed path becomes the turf edge. Any other paths or single points become spot shots for cut and fill.

Elevations

Shoot the corners first. Corner 1 is your zero. Enter the others in inches above it (+) or below it (−), like -3.5, or feet and inches like -1'2. Leave a corner blank if you didn't shoot it.

Border elevations

Pull a tape along an edge from a corner, shoot the elevation, and enter it here. Or turn on Tap border and tap the edge on the plan; it snaps to the line and shows the distance.

Extra spot elevations

Add these where the ground dips or humps between corners. Measure each spot's distance from corner 1 along X and Y.

In the playground layout app: Tapes, Points, Copy playground file. Paste it here. The playground border drops onto your turf rectangle and every footing gets its grade.

Corner 1 is the zero (the first corner on the plan above). Leave the two "in from" boxes at 0 when your turf rectangle is the playground border.

    Footing grades

    Elevations in inches from corner 1. Ground is what's there now, Finish is the finished grade from the Grade tab, +/− is fill (+) or cut (−) at the hole. Depth is the plan depth from finished surface; Dig is how deep to dig from the ground that's there now. Posts are bold.

    Send grades to the layout app

    Copies the ground, finished grade and dig depth at every hole, plus a grade map of the whole border. In the layout app: Tapes, Points, Grades from the turf app, paste.

    Shots from the layout app

    Elevation shots you added on the layout map (orange F1, F2…). In the layout app: Layout map, ⛰ Elev, Copy shots for turf app. Paste here. Shots on the border line come in as border points, the rest as spot shots. Pasting again updates them.

    Grade, cut & fill

    Set the finished turf grade. The app digs the base depth below it and tells you how much dirt comes out or goes in. Drag the model to turn it.
