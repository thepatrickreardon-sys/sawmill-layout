    Before you pour

    • Set a brick or block under each post so it can't sink while you plumb and level.
    • Pour only after the structure is assembled, braced, plumb and level.
    • Concrete within 4" of the top of the hole, sloped away from the post. Wash splashes off the posts.
    • 48 hours cure before anyone plays on it (Rock-N-Wave slab: 72 hours, 7 days best).

    Offset string line

    A string set a little off the pole centers so you can square the outside of the poles when you pour. Measure the offset from the pole center or from the outside edge of the pole; 2½" off center is the outside face of a 5" pole. Enter the holes on one line, or tap a hole on the map and add it.
